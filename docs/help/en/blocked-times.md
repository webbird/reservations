# Blocked periods

You enter holidays, maintenance or public holidays as an ordinary booking with a blocking status. There is no separate management for blocked periods, and none is needed.

## Block a period

1. Click **Bookings** at the object in the object list.
2. Enter the period.
3. Choose the status **Blocked**.
4. Enter the reason under **Internal note** if needed. Click **Save**.

The status "Blocked" only shows "occupied" on the website, without a reason. A public label does not appear with it.

## Block several objects

Blocked periods apply per object. To block several objects, add the booking for each one.

> [!NOTE]
> Regularly closed times such as weekends or lunch breaks do not belong here. By period, use the [opening hours](opening-hours.md) for them.
