# Reservations help

Reservations shows on your WBCE site when holiday flats, rooms or devices are occupied, and accepts booking requests. This help explains how to set up and maintain the module in the backend.

## Five steps to your first calendar

1. **Add a page** A new page with the module "Reservations". Each section is a calendar of its own. [Set up a section](section-setup.md)
2. **Choose the settings** Single object or several objects, booking by day or by period. [Settings](settings.md)
3. **Adapt the statuses** Requested, Reserved, Booked, Blocked and others are already there. Colours and patterns as you like. [Statuses](statuses.md)
4. **Add objects** A flat, a room or a device, with number of persons, lead time and opening hours. [Objects](objects.md)
5. **Switch on requests** A form below the calendar, with a mail to you and an automatic retention period. [Requests](requests.md)

## Which booking mode fits?

The mode applies to all objects of a section. For objects with the other mode, add a second section.

- **By day:** arrival and departure, including changeover days. For holiday flats and rooms to stay in.
- **By period:** date and time, with a grid and opening hours. For meeting rooms and devices.
